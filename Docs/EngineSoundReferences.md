## Facts found that feed the model
- Senior MAX EVO exhaust valve: ECU opens it at a driver-selectable **7,600 or 7,900 rpm** (DD2: 8,800 / 9,100).
  Secondary source (KartPulse Q&A / dealer pages), not the Rotax manual. Applied: EngineSound ValveOpenRpm = 7,600
  (was an 8,400 ASSUMPTION). Manual section to confirm it: manualslib Rotax 125 MAX "Setting of the exhaust valve timing".
- Licensed recordings: no Rotax kart recording was found on Freesound. Commercial libraries (A Sound Effect, Soundstripe)
  sell kart / 2-stroke packs; they're an option for licensed audio.
